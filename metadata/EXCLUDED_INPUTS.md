# Excluded inputs

The following materials are intentionally not included:

1. Manuscript files (`main.tex`, manuscript PDF, journal class/style files,
   bibliography build products, and manuscript build scripts).
2. The non-authoritative historical
   `T6_LYAPUNOV_CHAOS_RAW_V1_0_3` NPZ namespace.
3. Intermediate validation branches that are not needed to reproduce the
   curated tables and figures.

The final Lyapunov tables are derived from
`t6_v1_0_6_96row_chaos_classification_results_v1_0_0.json`.
