# Literature context for the archived products

The archived products are intended to support analyses of Milky Way globular
cluster orbits, tidal histories, and finite-time orbital chaos. Relevant
methodological context includes Gaia-era cluster kinematics, Galactic mass
models, tidal-tensor methods, tidal-shock theory, tidal-tail dynamics, and
chaotic dispersal of tidal debris.

The reproducibility archive does not contain the manuscript or its bibliography.
